Research clusters secure by default, without slowing researchers down

On a 2026 contract I assessed and strengthened data-access security on a university HPC cluster used for CFD research. The client's systems stay confidential; this is the method I bring.

IAMcentral identity & access
MFAstrong authentication
IaCevery change in Ansible

The problem

Shared research clusters grow over years: accounts, permissions and ways in pile up, and security tends to compete with researchers who just need their jobs to run. Lock things down without care and people find workarounds; leave them open and the data is exposed.

How I work

Assess. Map who uses the cluster, which data they need, and every way in; rank each risk by severity so effort goes where it matters.

Harden. Keep administration apart from user access, move identity to a central LDAP directory with encrypted lookups (SSSD), enforce multi-factor authentication and one password policy, and give project data least-privilege permissions.

Keep it usable. Offer a browser way in (Open OnDemand), so the secure path is also the easy one.

Codify. Write every access setting as version-controlled Ansible, so any change is tracked and any node can be rebuilt to a known, audited state.

Document. Hand over a technical report with findings, measures and residual risks, plus operating procedures for users and administrators.

Result

For the client: an assessment of where the risks were, the measures put in place, and the procedures to keep the cluster secure after I leave. The details of their systems are confidential under contract; I'm happy to walk through the method in a call.
